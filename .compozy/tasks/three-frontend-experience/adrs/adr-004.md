# ADR-004: Keep Capture Submission Immutable and Separate from Result Review

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-07

## Context

Capture serves people who receive a scoped link to register origin photos, complete an inspection, or answer a recapture request. Its participants may not have tenant accounts, and their authorization must not expose Dashboard or Admin data. Allowing post-submission editing would weaken evidence lineage and blur the boundary between evidence collection and result review.

## Decision

Capture remains an invitation-scoped, mobile-first product for three journeys: origin capture, inspection capture, and directed recapture. Link and OTP establish access only to the assigned responsibility.

Submission makes the evidence set immutable. The participant sees confirmation only and cannot reopen, alter, or browse the completed submission. Any correction creates a new directed recapture that preserves the prior evidence and replacement lineage. Progress before submission survives interruption according to the existing product guarantees.

Result tracking and published evidence review belong exclusively to Dashboard accounts with explicit permission.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Permit Editing Until Analysis Starts

- **Description**: Let participants reopen a completed submission while analysis is pending.
- **Pros**: Simple correction of accidental omissions.
- **Cons**: Creates an unstable evidence set and timing-dependent behavior.
- **Why rejected**: Directed recapture provides correction without rewriting history.

### Alternative 2: Combine Capture and Customer Review

- **Description**: Let invitation recipients return through the capture link to monitor progress and results.
- **Pros**: No customer account needed for follow-up.
- **Cons**: Expands a short-lived responsibility credential into a result portal and risks exposing internal data.
- **Why rejected**: Dashboard is the permissioned review product; Capture must remain narrowly scoped.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Evidence history remains inspectable and reproducible.
- Invitation access cannot become a path into customer or internal reporting.
- The capture experience stays focused and understandable on mobile devices.

### Negative

- Correcting a completed submission requires a new invitation and participant action.
- Participants cannot use the original link as a status tracker.

### Risks

- Participants may expect to edit after submission; confirmation copy must explain finality before the last action.
- Interrupted uploads could be mistaken for submission; completion must never be claimed before the server accepts it.

## Implementation Notes

The Technical Specification must preserve existing scoped-session, CSRF, resumability, media verification, and recapture guarantees while isolating Capture from Admin and Dashboard sessions.
