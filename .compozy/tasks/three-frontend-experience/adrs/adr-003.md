# ADR-003: Make Customer Publication Policy Tenant-Configurable

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-07

## Context

Customer users need progress and published results without seeing unfinished internal analysis. Some tenants require a deliberate internal release step; others want every final report to become available immediately. The product must support both operating models without obscuring the advisory nature of automated findings.

## Decision

Admin lets each tenant choose one report-publication policy:

- **Manual**: an authorized internal user explicitly publishes a final report.
- **Automatic**: every final report is published immediately, including reports classified as critical or inconclusive.

Manual is the default for tenants without an explicit choice. Published critical and inconclusive reports remain clearly labeled as advisory and display their current state without softening or hiding it.

Customers receive configured channel notifications and an in-product notification for relevant progress changes and newly published reports. Notification previews contain no sensitive evidence or internal notes.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Manual Publication Only

- **Description**: Require internal review before every customer release.
- **Pros**: Maximum editorial control.
- **Cons**: Creates avoidable delay for tenants that accept immediate publication.
- **Why rejected**: Tenants need control over their own review and communication model.

### Alternative 2: Automatic Publication with Exception Holds

- **Description**: Publish routine reports automatically but hold critical or inconclusive reports.
- **Pros**: Adds a safety check for exceptional outcomes.
- **Cons**: Contradicts the confirmed requirement that automatic mode publish every final report.
- **Why rejected**: The chosen policy treats finality, not classification, as the publication trigger.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Tenants can align publication with their governance model.
- Customers receive timely results in automatic mode.
- Manual mode preserves an explicit internal release decision.

### Negative

- Automatic publication may expose a critical or inconclusive result before a person discusses it with the customer.
- Users must understand the difference between final analysis and advisory interpretation.

### Risks

- A tenant may enable automatic publication unintentionally; Admin must explain the consequence before saving.
- Notifications could disclose sensitive detail; they must carry only safe status and navigation context.
- A later policy change could be applied ambiguously; it affects future finalizations only unless the administrator explicitly publishes an existing report.

## Implementation Notes

The Technical Specification must define publication state, authorization, notification integration, and the point at which a finalized report becomes customer-visible.
