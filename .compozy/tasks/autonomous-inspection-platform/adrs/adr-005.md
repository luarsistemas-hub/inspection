# ADR-005: Use Scoped, Passwordless External Participation with Directed Recapture

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-02

## Context

Tenants, property owners, construction participants, and cleaning participants need to capture evidence without dashboard accounts. The process must be easy to enter while remaining restricted to one inspection. Quality issues may only become apparent after submission.

## Decision

Use an individual expiring link plus OTP for each external capture responsibility, including origin creation. The team selects one or more verified delivery channels; invitations, reminders, and recapture requests go to all selected channels, and the same OTP goes to all selected channels with shared expiry and attempt limits. Each inspection has one external evidence contributor. After submission, the participant sees only confirmation and cannot review evidence, findings, classification, or reports. The system or an authorized internal user may request directed recapture with a reason. A new controlled access reopens only the affected requirements, preserves every prior submission, and closes at correction or deadline. Deadline expiry finalizes with available evidence and at least `ATTENTION`.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Dashboard Accounts for Every Participant

- **Description**: Require OIDC-backed accounts for external capture.
- **Pros**: Familiar identity and session management.
- **Cons**: Adds onboarding friction for occasional participants.
- **Why rejected**: Link and OTP without account creation are core to the autonomous journey.

### Alternative 2: Irreversible Submission

- **Description**: Never allow evidence correction after submission.
- **Pros**: Simpler lifecycle and strong finality.
- **Cons**: A single unreadable or incorrect image can make the inspection inconclusive.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected directed recapture by system or internal reviewer.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Occasional participants can contribute without account setup.
- Corrections target only deficient evidence and retain chain of custody.
- Multi-channel delivery reduces missed invitations.

### Negative

- Sending the same OTP to every selected channel reduces separation between link and code.
- Participants cannot independently retrieve what they submitted after confirmation.

### Risks

- Forwarded link and channel access may let another person act for the invitee; mitigate with audit data and explicit participant responsibility.
- Repeated correction requests may burden participants; mitigate with due dates and reason visibility.

## Implementation Notes

Token format, rate limiting, and provider adapters belong in the TechSpec. Product behavior requires the token to expose only its inspection or origin-capture scope.
