# ADR-004: Keep AI Reports Internal, Immutable, and Advisory

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-02

## Context

Image comparison can reduce manual review effort, but the resulting findings may be incomplete or wrong and may affect a tenant, contractor, or service provider if treated as an automatic decision. The MVP intentionally excludes report approval, assignment, commenting, and operational resolution workflows.

## Decision

Use AI findings and deterministic classification only for internal triage. The product never applies charges, liability, penalties, maintenance actions, or participant communications automatically from a report. External participants do not see findings, classification, or reports. Every completed inspection produces an immutable report version, including when analysis fails permanently. Consolidated reports create a new immutable version after each completed stage, display the latest state and currently observable pendencies as the headline classification, and preserve prior classifications in a timeline. New `CRITICAL` findings trigger configurable alerts to selected internal recipients only.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Human-Confirmed Operational Actions

- **Description**: Let a reviewer approve findings and initiate charges or work orders.
- **Pros**: Extends the product from triage into operations.
- **Cons**: Requires dispute, approval, accountability, and workflow features outside the confirmed MVP.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected internal triage only.

### Alternative 2: Automated Consequences

- **Description**: Allow classifications to trigger participant-impacting actions without review.
- **Pros**: Maximum automation.
- **Cons**: High risk from false findings and opaque automated decisions.
- **Why rejected**: It conflicts with the advisory role of AI and the user's explicit choice.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Internal users retain responsibility for consequential decisions.
- Reports remain reproducible and auditable.
- Permanent analysis failure does not prevent a usable report.

### Negative

- The MVP does not close the loop on identified problems.
- Internal teams must act outside the product after triage.

### Risks

- Users may still over-rely on AI language; mitigate with evidence, confidence, inconclusive states, and advisory labeling.
- A latest-state headline may hide past severity; mitigate with a visible stage timeline.

## Implementation Notes

Physical AI providers and model aliases belong in the TechSpec. The product contract requires structured, schema-valid results and deterministic classification outside the model prompt.
