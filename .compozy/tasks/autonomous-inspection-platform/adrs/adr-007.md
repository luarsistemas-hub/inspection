# ADR-007: Apply Hierarchical Tenant and Business-Unit Access

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-02

## Context

Inspection tenants may contain multiple business units, assets, staff members, and external participants. Internal users need access proportional to their responsibility, and no URL, query, event, media access, or report may expose another tenant's data.

## Decision

Enforce tenant isolation on every user-visible operation and apply hierarchical internal access. `TENANT_ADMIN` can administer the entire tenant. `MANAGER` can operate assigned business units. `EMPLOYEE` can operate assigned assets or inspections. `VIEWER` has read-only access to assigned business units. External participants can access only the capture responsibility granted by their active invitation. Invalidated inspections remain visible to authorized users but are excluded from valid-inspection indicators.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Tenant-Wide Visibility for All Internal Users

- **Description**: Vary actions by role but expose every tenant asset to every internal user.
- **Pros**: Simpler navigation and permission model.
- **Cons**: Reveals unrelated unit and participant data.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected hierarchical scope.

### Alternative 2: Individual Assignment for Every Role

- **Description**: Restrict even administrators to explicitly assigned assets.
- **Pros**: Maximum least privilege.
- **Cons**: Prevents tenant-wide administration and creates high assignment overhead.
- **Why rejected**: Tenant administrators need full tenant governance.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Permissions match organizational responsibility.
- Business-unit managers can work independently without cross-unit exposure.
- Read-only users cannot mutate inspection state.

### Negative

- Reassigning units, assets, and staff requires careful access updates.
- Users may see empty states when they have a role but no assignment.

### Risks

- A missed authorization boundary could leak sensitive evidence; mitigate with isolation tests across every access path.
- Stale assignments may preserve access too long; mitigate with immediate scope recalculation and audit.

## Implementation Notes

Authentication provider and authorization enforcement mechanisms belong in the TechSpec. The product requirement applies equally to APIs, media links, notifications, exports, and background processing.
