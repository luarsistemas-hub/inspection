# ADR-001: Use a Declarative Multi-Segment Inspection Product Model

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-02

## Context

The product starts with autonomous periodic property inspections but must also support construction progress and quality inspections and cleaning execution and quality inspections. The segments share evidence capture, comparison, scheduling, analysis, and reporting, while differing in participant roles, asset fields, capture stages, and reference modes. The existing repository does not provide a reusable inspection domain.

## Decision

Build one multi-tenant inspection product whose segment and versioned template declare participant roles, asset-specific fields, capture requirements, comparison mode, stage behavior, GPS policy, and report presentation. The supported comparison modes are fixed origin, planned stage, previous inspection, before/after, and checklist without a visual reference. Multi-stage capture and consolidated-versus-historical report presentation are independent template flags available to every segment.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Property-only Product

- **Description**: Limit the MVP to periodic rental-property inspections.
- **Pros**: Smaller initial domain and fewer persona variations.
- **Cons**: Does not meet the confirmed construction and cleaning scope or validate product extensibility.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly included construction and cleaning in the same PRD.

### Alternative 2: Separate Product Flow per Segment

- **Description**: Give property, construction, and cleaning independent behavior and user journeys.
- **Pros**: Each segment can be optimized without shared abstractions.
- **Cons**: Duplicates common behavior and makes every new segment a software project.
- **Why rejected**: The product must introduce segments through configuration when existing capture interactions suffice.

## Consequences

### Positive

- New segments can reuse proven capture and evidence behaviors.
- Segment-specific roles and comparison semantics remain explicit.
- Product rules are versioned and reproducible for each inspection.

### Negative

- The generic renderer and template validation must cover materially different journeys.
- Configuration errors can affect a full segment unless publication is validated.

### Risks

- A generic model may hide segment-specific needs; mitigate by allowing explicitly identified specialized components when existing interactions are insufficient.
- Template flexibility may confuse tenant users; mitigate with curated, validated templates rather than an MVP visual editor.

## Implementation Notes

The TechSpec must reconcile this product model with the repository's Vertical Slice Architecture. The PRD does not prescribe package layout or storage design.
