# ADR-006: Enforce Configurable Retention with Fixed Default Periods

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-02

## Context

Inspection photos, reports, location, authentication data, and notification logs contain personal or operational information with different useful lifetimes. Tenants need predictable retention while the platform needs explicit defaults. The user chose not to allow deletion before the configured period ends.

## Decision

Let each tenant configure retention by data class. By default, retain media, inspections, and reports for five years after the related occupancy, service relationship, or project closes; retain operational records and separately usable location data for one year; retain invitation and OTP secrets only until expiry. Do not delete data early on request. Record the request and explain the active retention restriction. Delete eligible data at the end of its configured period with an auditable outcome.

Before capture, require explicit participant acceptance of the disclosed use of photos, AI analysis, and GPS. Refusal blocks participation. Photos containing faces, documents, or other identifiable personal information are prohibited. Detection blocks submission unless the participant declares a false positive; that declaration and the photo are flagged for internal review.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Immediate Deletion on Request

- **Description**: Delete all related data whenever a participant requests it.
- **Pros**: Maximizes immediate individual control.
- **Cons**: Can remove inspection evidence during an active relationship or required retention period.
- **Why rejected**: The user chose no deletion before configured expiry.

### Alternative 2: Indefinite Retention

- **Description**: Keep evidence while the tenant account remains active.
- **Pros**: Preserves all history.
- **Cons**: Accumulates sensitive data without a defined purpose horizon.
- **Why rejected**: It conflicts with configurable minimization and explicit defaults.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Tenants understand when each class of data expires.
- The participant receives a clear disclosure before capture.
- Accidental sensitive content has an explicit prevention and review path.

### Negative

- Early deletion requests cannot be fulfilled within the product policy.
- False-positive declarations introduce material requiring internal review.

### Risks

- Configured periods or the no-early-deletion policy may conflict with applicable legal obligations; mitigate through legal validation before production and clear controller responsibilities.
- Automated sensitive-content detection may be inaccurate; mitigate through participant false-positive declaration and audit.

## Implementation Notes

The TechSpec must distinguish expired secrets from retained audit facts and define deletion behavior across derived media and report artifacts.
