# ADR-001: Focus the workflow on checklist, descriptive report, and move-out comparison

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-11-03

## Context

The source study covers the checklist/report problem and a wider product roadmap. The user selected the complete checklist, descriptive report, and move-in/move-out comparison journey for this workflow, and asked that the other opportunities remain visible for later work.

## Decision

This PRD covers scope definition, capture coverage, descriptive analysis, review, automatic versioned reports, and comparison against an approved baseline. It records the remaining roadmap opportunities as deferred follow-up work, without creating implementation tasks for them here.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Cover the entire source study

- **Description**: Include branding, account guidance, signatures, privacy, usage, and referrals in this workflow.
- **Pros**: One broad product plan.
- **Cons**: These capabilities have different product decisions and dependencies.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly chose the focused workflow.

### Alternative 2: Cover only current-state checklist

- **Description**: Leave comparison to another workflow.
- **Pros**: Smaller domain boundary.
- **Cons**: Coverage of the entry inspection would lack its intended future comparison outcome.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly included move-in/move-out comparison.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One connected behavior contract runs from capture to report and later comparison.

### Negative

- The surrounding product roadmap needs separate PRDs later.

### Risks

- Deferred opportunities may be mistaken for committed tasks; the PRD separates them explicitly.

## Implementation Notes

The task graph must contain only this PRD's active scope.
